name: requirements-feasibility
description: Analyze complex product requirements, feasibility, scope, assumptions, risks, and measurable acceptance criteria.
allowed_capabilities: [filesystem, shell]
---
Start from the final user outcome. Enumerate workflows, inputs, outputs, constraints, dependencies, assumptions, risks, and measurable acceptance criteria. Separate a runnable MVP from production-grade capability. For ML projects, identify required checkpoints, datasets, licenses, hardware, and quality metrics. Never hide an unavailable prerequisite behind a mock. Write decisions so downstream specialists can implement without redoing discovery.
