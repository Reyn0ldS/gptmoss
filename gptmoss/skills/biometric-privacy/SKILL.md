Minimize collected media and derived biometric data. Require explicit purpose and consent, define retention/deletion, isolate project data, avoid logs containing media or secrets, and document access boundaries. Validate uploads and metadata before processing. Distinguish a neutral canonical avatar representation from sexualized or identity-sensitive output. Turn every identified risk into an implementable control and a testable requirement.
