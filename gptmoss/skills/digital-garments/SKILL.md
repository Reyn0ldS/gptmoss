Treat garment reconstruction and body fitting as separate contracts. Capture category, dimensions, anchors, material assumptions, topology, and confidence. Define how a garment maps to multiple canonical bodies, including scale, deformation, collision, and failure cases. A silhouette-based deterministic prototype must be described honestly; photorealistic reconstruction requires validated model assets. Test cross-avatar repeatability and invalid garment inputs.
